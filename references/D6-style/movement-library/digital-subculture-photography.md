### Retro 90s Anime（1985–1999 | Japan）

> Film grain, soft color cast, absurdly intricate mechanical design — the texture of the OVA era.

- **Core claim**: film grain and a slight color cast (toward cyan or magenta); high-density mechanical and urban detail (the cyberpunk OVA tradition); soft atmospheric perspective and layered city skylines; the golden combination of cel coloring and hand-painted backgrounds
- **Composition**: complex urban depth, mechanical close-ups, figures placed in huge environments
- **Light and color**: color — a cool cyan cast or a warm brown cast, overall soft and not harsh; light — soft natural light plus neon reflection; film grain over the whole frame
- **Texture**: brushwork — slightly coarse line art, flat color blocks; extremely detailed backgrounds; materials — cels, hand-painted backgrounds, 35mm film grain, CRT playback texture
- **Emotion**: nostalgia, melancholy, urban solitude, the poetry of technology
- **Color anchors**: `#3A5A6B` `#8B5E2A` `#E85D75` `#2B2B33` `#C9A227` `#5A7A8B`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily comes out as modern anime — remedy: add 35mm film grain, retro colour cast, cel animation; 2) background density is easily lost — remedy: specify detailed hand-painted city background
- **Related movements**: Anime Cel, Cyberpunk, Vaporwave, movement overview

### Y2K Aesthetic（1997–2004 | Global）

> The optimistic imagination of the future: clear plastic, chrome, bubble type, and bright blue.

- **Core claim**: the millennium's optimism and naivety about future technology; a material cult of clear plastic, chrome, and jelly; bright blue, silver, white, fluorescent color; high gloss; bubble type, rounded interfaces, early digital graphics
- **Composition**: centered product-style, floating elements, rounded geometry
- **Light and color**: color — bright blue, silver white, fluorescent green / pink; high gloss and high saturation; light — strong specular highlights, iridescent refraction, bloom halos
- **Texture**: brushwork — zero brushwork; 3D rendering, metallic gradients, transparent materials; materials — clear plastic, chrome, jelly rubber, holographic film, iMac colored shells
- **Emotion**: optimistic, shiny, naive, the exhilaration of a tech utopia
- **Color anchors**: `#29D6E8` `#C9CFD4` `#A8F23D` `#F23D8E` `#FFFFFF` `#7B3FA0`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily comes out too dark and too modern — remedy: customize the description to the frame's intent and avoid it; 2) confusing it with Vaporwave — remedy: Y2K is brighter, more optimistic, more product-like; Vaporwave is darker and more ironic
- **Related movements**: Vaporwave, Memphis Design, Pixel Art, Synthwave

### Wabi-Sabi（1400–present | Japan）

> Beauty in imperfection and impermanence: cracks, rust, asymmetry, and an object worn by use.

- **Core claim**: wabi (rustic solitude) plus sabi (the traces of time); asymmetrical, imperfect, incomplete, unpretentious; kintsugi: repairing cracks with gold so the scar becomes the highlight; plain materials and natural aging
- **Composition**: minimal, asymmetrical, generous negative space, a single object centered or off-center
- **Light and color**: color — extremely low-saturation earth tones: gray-brown, moss green, rust, faded off-white; light — soft natural side light stressing the relief of texture; deep shadows left in
- **Texture**: brushwork — the material itself is the brushwork: clay grain, wood grain, rust spots, cracks; materials — coarse ceramic, rusted iron, weathered wood, linen, washi, kintsugi lacquer
- **Emotion**: stillness, sabi, humility, the gentleness brought by time
- **Color anchors**: `#8A7A6A` `#6B7A5A` `#A8432A` `#D9CFC0` `#C9A227` `#3A3632`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily gains gloss and newness — remedy: address that defect explicitly in the frame description; 2) it easily becomes an ordinary still life — remedy: state the opposite frame features explicitly
- **Related movements**: Japanese Ink Painting (Zen), Ink Xieyi, Minimalist Design

### Pixel Art（1980–present | Global）

> Every pixel is a decision: drawing a recognizable world with very few cells.

- **Core claim**: a low-resolution grid as an expressive medium rather than a limitation; a limited palette (often 8–32 colors) with dithering; the isometric viewpoint as the classic presentation; shaped by the hardware limits of the 8/16-bit game era
- **Composition**: isometric or orthographic viewpoint, tile repetition, a clear sense of grid
- **Light and color**: color — a limited indexed palette, high-saturation and mutually resonant; dithering producing intermediate tones; light — lighting rendered in stepped color blocks, no gradient; grid-aligned shadow
- **Texture**: brushwork — hard-edged pixel squares; outlines usually a single dark pixel; materials — digital pixel grid, CRT scanlines, indexed palette
- **Emotion**: nostalgia, precision, cute or tense depending on subject
- **Color anchors**: `#2B2B33` `#5A6B7A` `#7FC8E8` `#5FA85F` `#F2C230` `#D98B8B`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily generates fake pixels (false squares) — remedy: customize the description to the frame's intent and avoid it; 2) inconsistent resolution is the most common problem — remedy: stress an even, grid-aligned layout
- **Related movements**: Retro 90s Anime, Y2K Aesthetic, Memphis Design

### Synthwave（2005–present | Internet culture）

> The resurrection of the 1980s sci-fi poster: neon sunset, grid horizon, chrome and laser.

- **Core claim**: the visual revival of 1980s action films and sci-fi posters; a giant neon sun plus striped horizon plus perspective grid; chrome type and laser outlines; sports cars, sunsets, palm trees, endless highways
- **Composition**: a level horizon at center, perspective grid extending outward, symmetrical sunset
- **Light and color**: color — magenta, cyan, orange-yellow sunset gradients; deep purple night sky; light — strong neon backlight, laser outlines, mirror reflections
- **Texture**: brushwork — zero brushwork: airbrush gradients plus hard-edge geometry; materials — digital airbrush, chrome, mirror, neon tube
- **Emotion**: speed, nostalgic exhilaration, the romance of a lonely night
- **Color anchors**: `#F23D8E` `#29D6E8` `#F2A93B` `#2B1B4A` `#8A8A85` `#F2C230`
- **Failure points**: 1) confusing it with Vaporwave — remedy: Synthwave is colder and harder, with a sense of speed; Vaporwave is softer and hazier; 2) the perspective grid is easily lost — remedy: state perspective grid, receding horizon in the composition
- **Related movements**: Vaporwave, Cyberpunk, Y2K Aesthetic, Art Deco

- **Composition**: centered subject, church ruin or graveyard setting, dramatic pose
- **Light and color**: color — black plus deep red / deep purple / silver white; high contrast, low saturation; light — hard single-source light, backlit silhouette, candle and moonlight
- **Texture**: brushwork — photography or finely detailed illustration, with strong material contrast; materials — lace, velvet, leather, silver jewelry, candle, stone carving

- **Color anchors**: `#0F0F12` `#6B1F2B` `#3A2A4A` `#B8B8B8` `#E8E4DA` `#8B1A2A`

- **Related movements**: Gothic Art, Dark Academia, Symbolism, Wet Plate Collodion

### Fantasy Art（19th century–present | Europe and America (illustration tradition)）

- **Composition**: heroic low angle or epic panorama, subject low and centered with its mass anchoring the frame
- **Light and color**: color — intense fire orange, purple, emerald green; high saturation, strong contrast; light — dramatic backlight, firelight, rim light; sources explicit and usually colored
- **Texture**: brushwork — impasto realism, high detail density, clearly differentiated materials; materials — oil on canvas, acrylic, digital painting
- **Emotion**: epic, adventure, the sublime and the dangerous together
- **Color anchors**: `#B4462A` `#6B3FA0` `#1F6B4A` `#C9A227` `#2B2B3A` `#E8DCC0`
- **Failure points**: 1) the setting easily piles up into an element hodgepodge — remedy: address that defect explicitly in the frame description; 2) anatomical breakdown is common — remedy: address that defect explicitly in the frame description
- **Related movements**: Romanticism, Symbolism, Surrealism, Neo-Romanticism

### Memphis Design（1981–1988 | Italy）

> 1980s rebellious design: deliberately ugly, deliberately clashing, deliberately not design.

- **Core claim**: deliberately breaking modernist rules and taste; geometric shapes collaged at random: squiggles, dots, triangles, zigzags; high-saturation clashes, deliberately discordant; plastic laminate and cheap materials given design value
- **Composition**: scattered elements, asymmetrical, deliberately crowded and unbalanced
- **Light and color**: color — high-saturation clashes: pink, cyan, yellow, black, white crudely juxtaposed; light — no light and shade, pure flat graphic
- **Texture**: brushwork — zero brushwork; geometric shapes and pattern fills; materials — plastic laminate, terrazzo, neon tube, metal mesh
- **Emotion**: playful, provocative, optimistic, deliberately cheap
- **Color anchors**: `#F23D8E` `#29D6E8` `#F2E33D` `#111111` `#FFFFFF` `#7B3FA0`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily comes out too harmonious — remedy: address that defect explicitly in the frame description; 2) the 1980s are not all Memphis — remedy: stress geometric shapes, squiggles, laminate
- **Related movements**: Y2K Aesthetic, Vaporwave, Pop Art, movement overview

### Polaroid & Film（1948–present | Global）

> Imperfect immediacy: color cast, light leak, soft focus, and a white border.

- **Core claim**: the chemical color cast of instant imaging (toward cyan, yellow, or magenta); low contrast, soft blown whites and gray-blacks; light leaks, halos, blurred edges, white-frame signature; the intimacy and nostalgic warmth of an everyday moment
- **Composition**: casual snapshot, loose framing, white border centered or with an edge of blank
- **Light and color**: color — faded low-saturation color, highlights tinged yellow, shadows tinged cyan or gray; light — soft blown highlights, halos, slight light-leak streaks
- **Texture**: brushwork — not applicable — film grain and chemical unevenness; materials — instant film paper, film grain, uneven chemical development
- **Emotion**: intimate, nostalgic, tender, a private memento of time
- **Color anchors**: `#8FA8B8` `#E8D5A8` `#C9A8A0` `#5A6B5A` `#E8E4DA` `#2B2B33`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily becomes a "retro filter" that is too clean — remedy: customize the description to the frame's intent and avoid it; 2) the white border is the Polaroid signature — remedy: specify white border frame
- **Related movements**: Cyanotype, Wet Plate Collodion, Cottagecore, Dreamcore

### Anime Cel（1980–present | Japan）

> Flat color blocks plus hard-edged shadow plus clean line art: a visual grammar imitated worldwide.

- **Core claim**: cel coloring: flat color blocks, shadows as hard-edged blocks; uniform black or dark outlines; backgrounds may be hand-painted realism, contrasting with flat-color figures; eyes and hair highlights rendered in a highly codified way
- **Composition**: character centered, many close-up shots, realistic background perspective
- **Light and color**: color — bright flat fill, uniform skin tone, hair highlights as fixed color blocks; light — two-level shading (light / dark) plus a little highlight; shadow boundaries crisp
- **Texture**: brushwork — even line art plus flat fill; no brushwork texture; materials — cels, digital flat fill, hand-painted watercolor background
- **Emotion**: bright, emotional, dramatic, youthful
- **Color anchors**: `#F2C9A8` `#3A5A9B` `#E85D75` `#F2E33D` `#5FA85F` `#1A1A1A`
- **Failure points**: 1) cel is easily confused with pixel / 3D — remedy: specify flat colour fill, hard-edged shadow blocks, clean line art; 2) the background must also be written as hand-painted background, or figures and environment fall out of step
- **Related movements**: Retro 90s Anime, Superflat, Ukiyo-e, movement overview

### Minimalist Design（1960–present | Global）

> Remove everything removable: a little color, one shape, a great deal of blank space.

- **Composition**: generous negative space, strict alignment, a single visual focus

- **Texture**: brushwork — zero brushwork; vector-clean edges; materials — matte paper, sans-serif type, smooth surface, pale timber

- **Color anchors**: `#FFFFFF` `#F2F0EC` `#D9D5CC` `#8A8A85` `#1A1A1A` `#C9A227`

### Wet Plate Collodion（1851–1880 | Europe and America）

> Exposure must be completed within the few wet minutes: so everyone had to hold still.

- **Core claim**: the collodion wet-plate process, requiring on-site coating and immediate development; long exposure leading to stiff posture and solemn expression; edge flow marks, chemical stains, vignetting as the process signature; extremely high resolution and a distinctive silver-gray tonality
- **Composition**: portraits mostly bust or full length, frontal or three-quarter, plain background
- **Light and color**: color — silver gray, dark brown, milky white; a single hue leaning cool or warm; light — hard light or soft north light; pronounced vignetting, chemical flow marks at the edges
- **Texture**: brushwork — not applicable — uneven coating, dust, and scratches form the texture; materials — glass or black aluminum plate, collodion, silver salt, varnish, metal frame
- **Emotion**: solemn, grave, the weight of time, a moment frozen
- **Color anchors**: `#3A3632` `#6B6560` `#9A938A` `#D9CFC0` `#2B2B2B` `#8B7A5A`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily becomes a clean retro filter — remedy: customize the description to the frame's intent and avoid it; 2) posture too natural — remedy: stress stiff pose, long exposure stillness
- **Related movements**: Cyanotype, Polaroid & Film, Gothic Subculture, movement overview

### Cyanotype（1842–present | United Kingdom / Global）

> Iron salts turn blue in light: the world's first process that let ordinary people print their own photographs.

- **Core claim**: iron cyanide photosensitivity, development in water, producing Prussian blue; contact printing: objects placed directly on the paper for exposure (photogram); only blue and white, with no middle gray or a very narrow gray range; botanical specimens, architectural blueprints, and body images as classic subjects
- **Composition**: flat arrangement (specimen-style), architectural-blueprint style, symmetrical
- **Light and color**: color — Prussian blue plus paper white; a single hue, tone distinguished by density; light — hard-edged contours produced by contact printing; no concept of depth of field
- **Texture**: brushwork — not applicable — coating marks and brush streaks become part of the image; materials — watercolor paper, iron salt coating, hand-coated brush marks, paper fiber
- **Emotion**: scientific, nostalgic, cool, the poetry of botany
- **Color anchors**: `#0F2A5C` `#1F4A8B` `#4A7AB8` `#8FAFD0` `#E8E4DA` `#D9CFC0`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily adds a blue filter over the whole image while keeping photographic texture — remedy: address that defect explicitly in the frame description; 2) there must be only blue and white — remedy: customize the description to the frame's intent and avoid it
- **Related movements**: Wet Plate Collodion, Polaroid & Film, Wabi-Sabi, movement overview

### Cyberpunk（1980–present | Global (originating in the US / Japan)）

> High tech, low life: neon, rainy night, giant ads, bodies devoured by capital.

- **Core claim**: the vertical metropolis: megastructures pressing down on narrow alleys; neon as the only key light, wet ground doing the reflecting; prosthetics, holographic ads, surveillance, and crowded throngs; the palette fixed by Blade Runner: cyan-blue plus magenta plus amber
- **Composition**: low angle on the megastructure, dense stacked signage, canyon-like street depth
- **Light and color**: color — cyan and magenta colliding, amber / orange as accents of human warmth, large areas of black; light — neon as key light, mirror reflection on wet surfaces, volumetric fog plus bloom, high contrast
- **Texture**: brushwork — depends on medium: impasto plus hard edges for concept design; clean realism for the cinematic look; materials — wet asphalt, chrome, holographic projection, LED, plastic, steam
- **Emotion**: alienation, oppression, hallucination, dangerous allure
- **Color anchors**: `#00E5FF` `#FF2D95` `#FFA53D` `#0A0E14` `#3B1F5E` `#8CFFE0`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily gives only a "neon city" and loses noir's oppression — remedy: customize the description to the frame's intent and avoid it; 2) the common error is turning it into daylight or into pure purple — remedy: stress night, cyan+magenta, wet reflections
- **Related movements**: Art Deco, Expressionism, movement overview, category index — Digital, subculture, and photographic aesthetics

### Film Noir（1940–1958 | United States / France）

> Black and white, rainy night, venetian-blind shadows: moral ambiguity turned into a structure of light.

- **Core claim**: high-contrast black-and-white photography, large areas of dead black; striped shadows cast by venetian blinds, railings, and smoke; low angles and dutch angles producing unease; the femme fatale, the private detective, a fatalism doomed to fail
- **Composition**: tilted framing, low angle, figures cut by shadow, mirrors and reflections
- **Light and color**: color — pure black and white, extreme contrast (blown highlights, fully black shadows); light — hard single-source light, blind shadows, light shafts in smoke, wet reflections of neon signs
- **Texture**: brushwork — not applicable — a photographic medium with pronounced grain; materials — black-and-white film grain, wet asphalt, smoke, rain, glass
- **Emotion**: fatalism, alienation, danger, the melancholy of moral ambiguity
- **Color anchors**: `#0A0A0A` `#E8E8E8` `#5A5A5A` `#2B2B2B` `#8A8A85` `#FFFFFF`
- **Failure points**: 1) it easily gains color — remedy: address that defect explicitly in the frame description; 2) insufficient contrast turns it into an ordinary black-and-white photo — remedy: state the opposite frame features explicitly
- **Related movements**: Ashcan School, Cyberpunk, Expressionism, movement overview
